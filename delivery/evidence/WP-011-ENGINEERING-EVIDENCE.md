## Interpretations and findings for Director review

1. **Uncovered collision (smallest example — the WP-009 A fixture):** when the reaction anchor high K equals (or exceeds) the frozen impulse B, a confirmation (close ≥ K+tick) necessarily contacts the narrative destination B first; MP-002 §3 then ends the scenario DESTINATION_REACHED before confirmation (birth 05:00, arm 05:15 with K = B = 100240, destination contact 05:20). Implemented literally; no private rule. This can remove A confirmations whose anchor sits at the impulse high.
2. Destination contacts are monitored from ARM (MP-002 retains the v0.2 spent-before-reaction precedence for WATCH; applying B-contact in WATCH would end almost every A at birth).
3. **Pre-existing v0.2 defect:** the dislocation baseline (`core._dislocation_update`) re-processes the older retained slot every dispatch and resets, so it never reaches 60 slots and the dislocation veto never applies in v0.2 (WP-009 and Backtest A included). v0.2 is kept byte-identical; v0.3 applies the MP-001 rule correctly — paired comparisons therefore also include this correction.
4. Confirmed-scenario family premise = the MP-001 §9 call premise (A close < impulse A, B < U−z, C < L−z); unconfirmed scenarios keep the v0.2 withdrawals.
5. Coverage for ARMED/CONFIRMED scenarios: a missing post-arm minute or 1m/15m staleness → UNASSESSABLE (coverage loss); WATCH unchanged.
6. Evaluation start keeps structural warmup scenarios/owners/box/latches as labelled context and clears every pending/WAIT child (v0.2 reset everything).
7. A return is evaluated on the latest newly admitted minute of a dispatch (never an older minute of a catch-up batch).
8. A call's limiting landmark is kept as a frozen copy only (v0.3 does not keep a call-referenced landmark eligible past its age), so cost-dependent calls cannot change the structural zone set.
9. Post-issue v0.3 A calls close current entry while the side price is inside an eligible opposing zone (later obstacle = current-entry restriction; outcome criteria unchanged).
10. Live: WAIT children existing at LIVE activation end (`CONFIRMED_BEFORE_LIVE_ACTIVATION_NOT_LIVE_ADVICE`); pre-activation scenarios cannot issue live (as v0.2).
11. Call progress midpoint with an odd microsecond span rounds up; cap activation ambiguity uses intervals with start < activation ≤ end.
12. Because V contact is processed before 15m closes, a confirmed/armed B is normally invalidated before any opposite far-edge close; the structural-episode box-retirement rule is implemented and tested at dispatch level.
