## 2. Three objects and their lifetimes

- A structural scenario/discovery owner fixes birth qualification, impulse A/B, birth S15/z, original source references and original two-hour setup deadline. It describes a conditional continuation toward B. Before confirmation its reaction can still form; owner survival alone is never a confirmed direction or entry signal.
- A local reaction anchor has a distinct revision/epoch under that owner: complete 15m source, R, K, V, publication time/cursor and ACTIVE/INVALIDATED/UNASSESSABLE/SUPERSEDED status. Only one is active. Old anchors remain immutable journal evidence, not active state or fresh discovery owners.
- The first valid structural confirmation freezes R/K/V and its identity. Thereafter the v0.3 confirmed scenario, child WAIT/IMMEDIATE attempt and issued call follow their separate existing rules. There is at most one confirmation and one consumed economic child budget per structural scenario; changing an anchor before confirmation does not create an economic retry.

Structural state can be WATCH (no active anchor), ARMED (active local anchor), CONFIRMED or TERMINAL. Losing the anchor returns the same unconfirmed scenario to WATCH with an explicit reason; it does not reset Q_A, create a new birth, grant a new owner, re-enable an already consumed child, or silently erase historical ARMED state. Records name the anchor epoch and historical publication.

## 3. Pre-confirmation local contact and replacement

For LONG an eligible complete trade minute wholly starting at/after the active anchor's actual publication, admitted after its cursor, with low <= V invalidates that anchor. SHORT reflects: high >= V. Inclusive equality is contact. Test all pertinent newly admitted complete intervals against the anchor active for their time domain before publishing derived revisions. Journal the contact and its interval/source. No confirmation or entry may use that invalidated anchor, including a recovery in the contact minute.

A minute straddling anchor publication cannot certify a post-publication contact. If its extrema can contact the anchor, mark that anchor UNASSESSABLE and unusable; record the temporal ambiguity. This alone does not certify failure of the entire unconfirmed structural scenario. A known required coverage/monitoring gap still terminates the scenario under §4. A fresh anchor must satisfy the same prospective replacement rules below; ambiguity is never a clean confirmation or hypothetical fill.

If the structural owner remains valid, a replacement is permitted only by a newly published complete, assessable 15m reaction satisfying the inherited MP-001 A predicates. It is not generated by a 1m recovery, quote, execution-cost change or return to the old K. The replacement uses the most recent reaction bar attaining the new R, K = that bar's high (LONG), V = R - frozen birth z; SHORT is the exact structural reflection. Preserve MP-001 R > A+z and R < B-0.25 birth S15, lower-clean-reaction recognition, allowed 1h context, no opposite EXPANSION and tie convention. A replacement after contact must be a strictly deeper clean R than the lost anchor's R (SHORT: strictly higher); no recycling the invalidated geometry. Its source bar's market end must be at/after the latest invalidating/ambiguous contact interval end, and it must be newly published at/after processing that evidence. An older retained bar cannot be republished to avoid a contact.

The source bar may be the very 15m bar that completes in the dispatch admitting the contact. Process the old contact first, then structural withdrawals/destination evidence and the complete bar, then publish the new anchor only if still valid. Its publication is the actual current dispatch, not the bar's historical end when receipt is delayed.

A later confirming minute must start at/after the replacement's actual publication and be admitted strictly later than its publication cursor, complete/fresh, with LONG close >= new K+tick and low > new V (SHORT reflects). The 15m source bar or an already admitted recovery never confirms the replacement. A dispatch admitting a backlog cannot use one of its earlier minute closes against an anchor just published at that dispatch. This prohibition is independent of pacing, checkpoints and UI frames.

Without contact, an inherited valid lower-clean-R revision supersedes the active old anchor and publishes a new epoch prospectively under the same confirmation cutoff. Anchor loss/revision is structural and identical under different execution-cost assumptions. The ancestor contact remains recorded even if a new anchor is published in the same dispatch.

## 4. Structural limits remain finite and unchanged

Before the first arm, retain MP-001 spent-before-reaction precedence: a high > B+z spends LONG before a qualifying reaction; reflect for SHORT. Losing an anchor does NOT return the scenario to the pre-first-arm spend phase: retain an ever_armed fact. No new spend predicate is introduced by this delta.

At every pre-confirmation dispatch terminate for original setup expiry, required evidence gap/staleness/restart gap, forbidden 1h context or opposite EXPANSION. Preserve the named complete-15m price boundary: LONG close <= frozen A+z withdraws; SHORT reflected close >= frozen A-z withdraws. This is a CLOSE predicate, not a new 1m low/high stop at A+z. The reaction eligibility condition R > A+z is separate: a bar failing reaction recognition does not automatically constitute that close withdrawal, nor authorize a wider anchor.

Preserve MP-002 narrative B contact after scenario publication and its certification/straddling rules. A certified destination terminal dominates any new anchor. A narrative destination contact simultaneous with local-anchor contact is conservatively scenario UNASSESSABLE, not certified success. Existing ambiguous-contact/data policy remains explicit; do not infer intrabar chronology.

Impulse A/B, birth S15/z, source identity, original deadline and qualification latch never move to sustain a reaction. No replacement after structural terminal; no new two-hour budget at anchor loss/replacement. Anchor loss does not release the discovery owner or retire its B zone. The structural release/reset rules from MP-002 remain, except that pre-confirmation LOCAL V loss alone is no longer structural release. After genuine structural release require the original post-release false then fresh false-to-true qualification; no banked pre-release false or same-dispatch true rebirth.

At the original deadline, expiry precedes all anchor recognition/confirmation/return gates. Owner release for the original deadline remains distinct from confirmed-scenario lifetime, as in MP-002.

## 5. Confirmation, WAIT and call protections unchanged

From the FIRST valid confirmation onward there is no R/K/V re-anchoring, including when geometry is empty, selection rejects, a WAIT exists without a call, or a call later retires. No new confirmation/child budget after rejection. Frozen V contact invalidates the confirmed scenario under MP-002; WAIT cannot return to reaction formation. Call-level protection/ambiguity, stop-gap accounting and evaluation cutoffs are untouched.

Preserve confirmation + 4h hard end for A, original setup deadline for WAIT, minimum residual, scenario/call progress origins, conservative target including own B zone, first-eligibility cap updates, fixed-cost economic predicates, IMMEDIATE/RETURN routing, one-slot joint selection, 60-second primary and all declared sensitivities. No extension of target, stop, costs, duration or corridor to fit October.

Bound by existing owner/deadline, no active-history growth.
