## 1. Product and epistemic boundary
The complete path is available evidence → factual/temporal state → observations → MarketView/scenarios → candidate → actionability → persistent call → reassessment/outcome. The output is BTC LONG/SHORT guidance for a human, never capital/quantity/leverage/orders. No LLM runs in the decision loop. No claim of professional equivalence, validated edge, calibrated probability or promised frequency is made before evaluation.
This is a coherent first translation, with three candidate families rather than an isolated signal or every trading style. Its intended domain is completed-bar opportunities lasting minutes to hours. It excludes instant news interpretation, order-book tactics and predictive fixed-period cycles from directional authority, while exposing their unavailable state and an explicit development path.

Classifications throughout: **SOURCE-CONCEPT** means a documented professional concept, not BTC profitability; **ADAPTATION** means our deterministic translation; **CONVENTION** means a frozen engineering/method hypothesis; **UNRESOLVED** names an exact missing decision/evidence. All numerical trading predicates below are conventions unless explicitly identified as source metadata. No existing RP-001 parameter is adopted by inheritance. Source dossiers and frozen cases remain unchanged; their legacy project prescriptions are not current rules.

## 3. Shared measurements and causal objects
Trade bars drive structure, triggers, price targets and evaluation predicates. Mark/index remain distinct reference series. Inputs are COMPLETE sealed temporal bars, except explicitly labelled 1m lifecycle evidence. Never use forming 15m bars for a setup or reinterpret late-excluded constituents.
For horizon H and completed bar j: TR_j = max(high-low, abs(high-previous_close), abs(low-previous_close)). `S_H` is median TR over 20 contiguous complete bars ending j; requires the preceding close as well. Zero scale is UNAVAILABLE. No floating-point rounding in predicates. Prices use the instrument metadata tick; conservative target/entry/stop rounding is specified in §8.
`ER_n = abs(C_j-C_(j-n)) / sum(abs(C_k-C_(k-1)))` over n transitions; zero denominator yields efficiency zero, not missing data if all inputs exist. Signed displacement is `(C_j-C_(j-n))/S_H`. Display raw measurements alongside categories.
Participation is trade base-volume ratio: latest completed 15m base volume / median previous 20 completed 15m base volumes. Median zero makes the ratio unavailable. Report contracts/base/quote distinctly. Volume is descriptive; no directional inference or compulsory high-volume gate in v0.1.

Reference objects:
- Completed previous UTC day/week/month extremes, if available; immutable as-known IDs.
- Confirmed 15m/1h pivots: a high must strictly exceed the highs of two complete bars on each side (low mirrors); known_at is the last right-hand bar's known_at. Ties yield no pivot. No lookahead/backdated drawing. Keep at most 64 eligible pivots per horizon; maximum age 24h (15m) / 7d (1h), with retirement explicit.
- A candidate's frozen setup range/extremes, not sliding levels after issuance.
- A zone around level P has half-width z = max(2 ticks, 0.10 × frozen S15). Price crossing alone does not automatically grant the opposite support/resistance role; retest behavior belongs to a candidate.
- An opposing pivot/period level is ineligible after a completed 15m close beyond its far edge, or retirement. Candidate selection uses the nearest eligible opposing near edge, never the most attractive distant target. Coincident levels retain distinct provenance; display grouping is cosmetic.
Each object has ID, version, event interval, known_at, admitted cutoff, dependencies, method/config hash and status. A provisional extreme is never a confirmed pivot. Gaps reset affected rolling windows/unfinished setup episodes; already issued calls are handled under §9, never repaired retroactively.

## 5. Continuous MarketView and cycle/phase
Recompute factual observations on their actual completed-bar cadence. Tactical trend on 1h: direction UP when signed 8-transition displacement ≥1.0 and ER8 ≥0.35; DOWN mirrors. Otherwise BALANCED. These are conventions, not predicted return. Unavailable inputs give UNAVAILABLE, never balanced.
Broad 4h/day descriptions are separate counterevidence; they neither vote nor mechanically veto tactical opportunities. Report location to known period/pivot levels and direction conflict explicitly. A candidate may be tactical counter to broad context and must say so.

Phase at each 15m close uses `CR = median(last 4 TR) / median(the preceding 20 TR)` (24 TR plus preceding close needed); `ER6` and signed six-transition displacement use current S15. Priority:
1. EXPANSION when ER6≥0.55 and abs(displacement6)≥1.5; direction from displacement.
2. COMPRESSION when CR≤0.75 and ER6≤0.35.
3. REACTION when tactical direction is UP/DOWN, net last-three-close change is counter to it, and EXPANSION is false.
4. ROTATION when ER8 on 1h≤0.25 and ER6≤0.35.
5. TRANSITION otherwise; UNAVAILABLE if required measurements are absent.
A phase change is published only when supported by newly complete input. Keep phase age, previous phase and measurements, not a supposed periodic clock. Compression licenses watching an exit; expansion is evidence of movement, not permission to chase; reaction licenses continuation assessment; rotation licenses a failure-test assessment. Changing phase does not erase existing calls.
This is the first source-grounded cycle implementation: **observed rest/movement phase and transition**, with decision effect. It does not detect accumulation/distribution intent, a cycle low before confirmation, a named long cycle or a universal expected next duration.

MarketView combines a conditional tactical path and explicit alternatives:
- UP/DOWN if tactical context is directional and no material required-data failure; explanation names pending continuation/exit conditions, nearest obstacle and invalidation of the view.
- BALANCED in rotation/compression without directional evidence; breakout alternatives are displayed on both sides.
- UNCERTAIN when an armed contrary scenario conflicts with the tactical path; no forced single direction.
- UNAVAILABLE when required context cannot be read.
View horizon is the next 1–4h scenario window, not a fixed 120-minute prediction. No probabilities. Separately score fixed-horizon directional labels only where their sampling/event definition is preregistered; a view alone never counts as a trade.

## 6. Three candidate episodes (long; short is exact price-axis mirror)
All extrema/levels and S15 freeze at episode creation; source bar IDs identify the episode. Each family produces one stable candidate per episode/direction, with append-only revisions before issue. No sliding-window re-arming spam. An arm event uses only complete bars; its trigger can use only subsequently admitted 1m bars whose market interval starts at or after the arming bar's end and whose publication is after the arm dispatch. Same-bar arm/trigger is forbidden.

### A. Continuation after reaction
Create an impulse episode at the first completed 15m bar where the last six-transition price path has ER6≥0.55, upward net displacement≥1.5 S15, and tactical 1h UP. Freeze impulse low A (minimum low of those seven endpoint bars), high B (maximum high), S15 and episode end. Do not require a delayed swing confirmation or epoch high-water breakout.
Watch the subsequent 1–8 complete 15m bars for a reaction: at least one lower close and reaction minimum R below B-0.25 S15 but strictly above A+z. If price makes a new high above B+z before any reaction, the episode is spent rather than endlessly extending its anchors.
Arm once a reaction bar exists and no opposite EXPANSION has appeared. The anchor is the most recent complete reaction bar attaining R; K=anchor.high, stop V=R-z. A lower reaction extreme before trigger can revise K/R/V if R>A+z and within the original 8-bar deadline. Never revise after issuance.
Trigger: next eligible complete 1m close ≥K+one tick, while its low>V; if the same bar touches V, withdraw as ambiguous/invalid rather than issue.
Target: nearest eligible opposing destination from §3, including frozen B's near edge; no destination gives NO_TARGET. The reward geometry may reject it, but it is not required to clear an arbitrary global former high-water mark. Expected holding label 30m–3h; absolute thesis deadline 4h from issue. Setup expires 2h from impulse end.

### B. Compression exit with retest
Create one frozen box [L,U] from highs/lows of the previous 16 complete 15m bars (excluding the current creation bar), when phase COMPRESSION, ER16≤0.35 and width between 2 and 6 S15. Need at least two different bars with highs within z of U and two with lows within z of L; if both edge conditions fail, no range claim. The creation bar must close inside the box. Box lives at most 4h; never slide its bounds.
A completed 15m close >U+z records an upward break; tactical 1h must not be DOWN. Do not issue on that bar. In the next four completed 15m bars require a retest: low intersects [U-z,U+z], close>U, and low>L. If a close<U-z occurs first, cancel the break attempt. A deep return through L destroys the box.
Arm at the first qualifying retest. K=max(U+one tick,retest.close); V=retest.low-z. Trigger is a later complete 1m close≥K+one tick with low>V. No retest/no room/no timely response means no call, not a chased breakout.
Destination is nearest eligible opposing landmark above entry; when none exists, permit only the explicit projected box-width objective U+(U-L), labelled PROJECTION rather than known resistance/calibrated forecast. A nearer landmark always caps it. Expected holding 30m–4h; hard thesis deadline 6h. Break/retest watch expires 1h after break; original box expiry still applies.

### C. Failed range exit back into rotation
Use a live frozen box from B, but only while tactical 1h BALANCED and local phase not directional EXPANSION. A completed 15m bar trades below L-z and closes back above L+z but below the box midpoint M. This is observed failure/re-entry, not a fact about trapped traders/liquidations. K=reclaim.close; V=reclaim.low-z; arm after its publication.
Trigger: later eligible complete 1m close>K+one tick with low>V. Initial target is min(M, nearest opposing eligible landmark above entry). No target beyond the midpoint is presumed. If a complete 15m close<L-z occurs before trigger, withdraw; a later directional expansion against the plan closes entry. Expected holding 15m–90m; hard thesis deadline 2h; untriggered watch expires 30m after reclaim.
No range fading merely on a touch. One C attempt per box/direction; a fresh failed-exit bar after a closed attempt is not a new episode unless a new frozen box is created after expiry/destruction.

## 7. Events, news, derivatives and execution
Typed EventContext records include stable ID/revision, scope (BTC/venue/macro), schedule time, schedule_known_at, publication time, content_known_at, provenance/version, coverage status and known cancellation/revision. These are separate from frozen marketdata/feed.v1 schemas. No runtime LLM parsing; curated deterministic event classifications are sufficient for first integration.
Initial scheduled scope: FOMC decision/press-conference times, US CPI and Employment Situation releases. Use timezone database America/New_York → UTC; no fixed UTC offset. Historical vintages or explicit conservative reconstruction are required. Current schedule cannot be assigned old known_at without proof. Schedules copied from a retrospective page yield RECONSTRUCTED_CALENDAR only; result mode must state this, never pretend strict as-known macro validity.
For a known event, prohibit new entry from 15m before to at least 15m after its declared time. After release require one entirely post-event complete 15m bar and a fresh scenario reassessment. Unknown actual content is not bullish or bearish. Observe price displacement, range/scale and reclaim/continuation; descriptive reaction is not event-causation proof. Press conference and decision are separate events; overlapping windows union. Timing windows are conventions.
Existing entry closes on the restriction; thesis remains inspectable with event-risk guidance, not a claim a person exited. High-severity admitted BTC/venue incidents close new entry until an explicit resolution record and fresh assessment. No comprehensive historical unscheduled-news coverage is claimed. General political/crypto news is UNKNOWN unless typed contemporaneous evidence exists; no automatic sentiment classifier is smuggled into v0.1.
Mark-trade and trade-index close differences on matched slots are reported in bps; excessive dislocation (>max(25bps,3×rolling median absolute difference over 60 complete matched slots)) restricts live execution adequacy, not direction. Missing match/history yields unavailable diagnostic; it cannot replace bid/ask. Funding never asserts crowded longs/shorts without positioning data. OI, liquidation prints, depth and aggressive flow are explicitly unavailable in the first method; their directional effect is NONE. Future additions need named incremental decisions and source tapes, not inferred signals.
Live actionability requires measured bid/ask no older than 5s, consistent instrument metadata and trade-bar freshness. Quote midpoint must be inside the permitted entry area; spread contributes to the adequacy envelope. Without quotes the app shows the candidate and WATCH/EXECUTION_UNVERIFIED, not ENTRY AVAILABLE NOW. Historical calls can have MODELED_EXECUTION_ADEQUACY under the registered cost envelope, unmistakably historical. Local stopped/disconnected operation closes ready-now entry; no live alerts promised while off.

## 8. Actionability, geometry and selection
Use direction coordinate d=+1 LONG/-1 SHORT; transform entry/target/stop distances so positive reward/risk has one formula. Initial entry area extends from trigger close toward stop by 0.25 S15, capped to keep the entire area strictly inside (V,T); upper adverse edge extends at most 0.25 S15 in trade direction. For LONG: [max(V+tick, trigger_close-0.25S15), min(T-tick,trigger_close+0.25S15)]; SHORT mirrors. If empty or current reference price outside it, WATCH/PRICE_OUTSIDE_AREA. Guidance is not a limit-order promise.
For price p in the frozen area: gross reward G=10000*d*(T-p)/p; gross risk Q=10000*d*(p-V)/p in bps. Let F be entry+exit modeled fees, A entry+exit spread/slippage allowance; Kcost=F+A. Normalized geometry adequacy is G>Kcost and (G-Kcost)/(Q+Kcost)≥1.2. This is a reward/risk convention, not an expected-value estimate, since outcome probabilities are uncalibrated.
Evaluation base assumption: 5bps fee and 2bps combined spread/slippage per leg (14bps round trip); stress 5bps fee +5bps allowance per leg (20bps). These are scenarios, NOT newly verified OKX/account fee rates, and must not be billed as empirical fills. Live quoted adequacy uses actual spread plus declared slippage envelope and explicit user/profile fee assumption; lack of a pinned fee assumption is UNVERIFIED. No account or trade-size lookup is required.
Funding future uncertainty is separately disclosed when the thesis crosses a settlement; known historical cashflows belong to evaluation after realization. A pending fee/cost change affects later entry reassessments, not original issued criteria silently.
Stops round away from entry to a valid tick; targets round toward entry; eligible entry prices and costs are recomputed after rounding. Scale and geometry at issue remain immutable. Do not move target farther away to pass room, widen stop after adverse movement or raise uncertainty to manufacture calls.
Among simultaneously actionable candidates: suppress same episode duplicates; opposite directions → CONFLICTED/no new call; same direction → deterministic priority A then B then C, then episode creation time, then ID. Priority is a convention, not empirical quality ranking. Retain the nonselected funnel records. At most one active call thesis for BTC; no silent replacement or inferred hedge. End the old thesis under its own criteria before publishing another. New opposite evidence may produce guidance before a replacement call. One call per episode prevents repeated notifications from identical evidence; no universal arbitrary daily trade quota.

## 9. Persistent lifecycle and timing
Candidate: WATCH → ARMED → TRIGGERED → issued/rejected/withdrawn/expired. Call has separate entry status AVAILABLE/CLOSED/UNVERIFIED and thesis status ONGOING/TARGET_REACHED/INVALIDATED/TIME_EXPIRED/UNASSESSABLE. Human position status is never inferred.
At issue freeze evidence/model/config, direction, geometry, episode source, horizon, clock/cursor, expected progress and original evaluation criteria. Versions record later explanation/entry/guidance changes append-only. IDs persist across refresh and restart.
Every fresh complete 1m trade bar reassesses: original target/stop contact, price inside entry area, remaining geometry, data/event restrictions and setup thesis. A target/stop contact in an entry-detection bar cannot be a post-issue result; published guidance existed only after that bar's close.
No entry closes just because one candle passed. It closes on target/stop, area/room failure, required stale/gap data, event restriction, family premise failure or hard thesis deadline. A merely out-of-area price can return while the thesis is valid: entry may reopen on a **new fresh assessment** if all frozen geometry/premise conditions hold and no terminal result has occurred; emit a material entry-state change under the same call, not a new call. Gap recovery does not prove unobserved target/stop safety; once thesis becomes UNASSESSABLE it does not reopen without a new episode and assessment.
Family premise failure: A complete 15m close below impulse A; B close below U-z after issue; C close below L-z after issue (SHORT mirrors). Premise failure publishes EXIT_GUIDANCE; normalized advisory exit follows separate evaluation timing rules. A later genuine target/stop observation remains auditable, but primary evaluation exits at the registered first valid exit event; no hindsight choosing the nicer outcome.
Progress checkpoint: at half the hard thesis horizon, compare last fresh close with issue reference; if no ≥0.5 S15 favorable displacement occurred on post-issue complete bars, publish STALLED and close entry, with reassessment/exit guidance. This is a declared hypothesis, not a forecast that the target must be reached by then. Do not infer MFE across missing bars. At hard deadline TIME_EXPIRED; do not force future outcomes beyond available clock_end.
No-event timers enforce freshness, event windows, progress/hard deadlines. Expiry precedes new publication at common clock time. Setup decisions at 15m/1h closes; material 1m lifecycle changes only, not one full SQL snapshot per minute. Live recorded dispatch receipts remain journalled; synthetic historical barriers never claim exact live-decision equivalence. Pacing/STEP/UI/checkpoint cadence cannot change semantics. Resume loads professional state directly alongside factual/temporal state; no default prefix replay.
On startup reconstruct obtainable history into a labelled catch-up phase; no retrospective actionable alerts. Previously pending/active calls whose monitoring gap overlaps their life become UNASSESSABLE with original evidence preserved. Current new calls require a fresh present dispatch, readiness and quotes. No warmup restart or live connection work is implemented by this document.

## 10. Semantic.v2 design (not a schema implementation)
Namespaced advisory records: Observation, Landmark, PhaseState, MarketView, Scenario, CandidatePlan, Actionability, AdviserCall, CallRevision, MaterialChange. Store bounded rolling windows, active episodes/calls/levels, pending timers, dedup keys and professional output commitments in the same fenced checkpoint ownership as replay state. Persist append-only material advisory events at semantic changes; not a full snapshot per input event. Complete trace and cutoff-safe inspection must remain available through event journal + checked restore points. Bounded state does not mean deleting call history. Journal/state format and validator scope/version require explicit implementation-spec decisions; no silent observe/feed/DEMO schema mutation.
Separate evaluation namespace links immutable issued call IDs/revisions to hypothetical entry/exit/outcome assumptions. No human wallet fields. semantic.v1 DEMO remains byte-identical.
