## 1. Product and epistemic boundary
The complete path is available evidence → factual/temporal state → observations → MarketView/scenarios → candidate → actionability → persistent call → reassessment/outcome. The output is BTC LONG/SHORT guidance for a human, never capital/quantity/leverage/orders. No LLM runs in the decision loop. Astra review and Director disposition: [review](MP-001-ASTRA-REVIEW.md), [disposition](MP-001-DIRECTOR-DISPOSITION.md). v0.1 is historical at commit cd8665a; v0.2 closes its eight specification findings without new market outcomes. No claim of professional equivalence, validated edge, calibrated probability or promised frequency is made before evaluation.
This is a coherent first translation, with three candidate families rather than an isolated signal or every trading style. Its intended domain is completed-bar opportunities lasting minutes to hours. It excludes instant news interpretation, order-book tactics and predictive fixed-period cycles from directional authority, while exposing their unavailable state and an explicit development path.

Classifications throughout: **SOURCE-CONCEPT** means a documented professional concept, not BTC profitability; **ADAPTATION** means our deterministic translation; **CONVENTION** means a frozen engineering/method hypothesis; **UNRESOLVED** names an exact missing decision/evidence. All numerical trading predicates below are conventions unless explicitly identified as source metadata. No existing RP-001 parameter is adopted by inheritance. Source dossiers and frozen cases remain unchanged; their legacy project prescriptions are not current rules.

## 3. Shared measurements and causal landmarks
Trade bars drive structure, triggers, targets and evaluation predicates. Mark/index remain distinct references. Setups use COMPLETE sealed 15m/1h bars; forming bars and late-excluded constituents never revise a past output.
For complete bar j at horizon H: TR_j=max(high-low,abs(high-previous_close),abs(low-previous_close)). S_H=median of 20 contiguous TR, requiring 21 complete bars. Zero scale is UNAVAILABLE. ER_n=abs(C_j-C_(j-n))/sum(abs(C_k-C_(k-1))) over n transitions; zero denominator gives zero efficiency if inputs are complete. Signed displacement=(C_j-C_(j-n))/S_H. All calculations Decimal/rational, never binary-float predicate tolerances.
Participation ratio=latest completed 15m base volume/median previous 20 completed 15m base volumes; zero baseline is unavailable. Contracts/base/quote remain distinct. Descriptive only: never claim volume confirmed a call when it had no decision effect. These price measurements are related facets, not independent votes.

### Landmark ownership
| Object | Birth and frozen zone | Eligibility/retirement |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed 15m/1h pivot high/low | Strict extremum above/below two complete bars on each side; ties produce none. known_at includes final right-hand bar. Freeze current S15 at creation, z=max(2 ticks,0.10 S15); no usable S15 → no zoned landmark yet. | High is opposing for LONG, low for SHORT. Retire 24h/7d from creation; cap 64 per horizon, oldest creation/ID first. Retained active-call refs cannot be deleted. |
| Previous completed UTC day/week/month high/low | When a period completes it becomes the previous-period landmark. Freeze S15 when first usable zoned object is created; if delayed, known_at is that later creation, never the earlier extremum time. | Retire when the next period at that horizon completes, or end-age exceeds 26h/8d/32d. Only immediately previous complete period, not a catalogue of six monthly targets. |
| A impulse B extreme | Birth with impulse episode; freeze episode S15/z. | High for LONG / low for SHORT; usable until episode ends. It is a destination, not an independently confirmed pivot. |
| B/C box edge | Birth of qualified compression box with frozen S15/z. | Same box owns L/U/M and lifetime. Attempt states do not move edges. |

All zones are symmetric [P-z,P+z], immutable from creation; resistance-high versus support-low is a locational type, not proven order flow. LONG near/far opposing edges are P-z/P+z (SHORT mirrors). A completed 15m close strictly beyond the far edge marks that opposing landmark BROKEN before target selection at the dispatch. Equality does not break it. No automatic role flip. A new pivot is a new object. A gap expires affected unfinished structural objects; no invented bridge.
A current trigger price inside an eligible opposing zone blocks entry as AT_OPPOSING_AREA, rather than ignoring the zone to reach a farther target. Fully passed/broken or retired objects are ineligible. Among eligible opposing near edges strictly ahead of trigger price, choose the nearest price; equal near edges tie by creation time then ID. Overlapping zones are not merged or widened; retain all provenance and choose by the same rule. Each zone has its own frozen width.
At a trigger, use the eligible landmark set after all newly complete derived updates/invalidation. Resolve and tick-round target then; freeze at issue. Pre-issue geometry revisions do not freeze an outdated target set. Rejected triggers cannot retry under a later better target in the same attempt. For B cap the projection by any nearer eligible landmark; C uses midpoint when no nearer opposing landmark exists. No economic significance is inferred from a landmark's mere eligibility.
IDs and envelopes include method/rule version, instrument, source bar/period IDs, event interval/extremum time, creation/known_at, cursor, frozen scale/zone, dependencies and status. Record target-limiting object's type/source/age in every rejection/call.

## 5. Context, phase and total MarketView/scenario function
At each supported close, compute 1h observed context UP when eight-transition signed displacement≥1.0 and ER8≥0.35; DOWN mirrors; otherwise BALANCED. Missing required data → UNAVAILABLE. Context is a measured path label, not itself a future forecast. 4h/day/week/month expose location relative to previous-period extremes and raw change, not unspecified votes/directions.
Local phase uses CR=median(last 4 TR)/median(the preceding 20 TR), ER6 and six-transition displacement/S15. Zero denominator/scale → UNAVAILABLE. Priority: EXPANSION (ER6≥0.55 and abs displacement≥1.5), COMPRESSION (CR≤0.75 and ER6≤0.35), REACTION (directional 1h and net three-transition close change counter to it), ROTATION (1h ER8≤0.25 and ER6≤0.35), otherwise TRANSITION. Direction of expansion is displacement sign. Phase has observed age and transition, not predicted turning dates.
**Family predicates in §6 are the sole entry authority.** Phase is an explicit gate for box birth and adverse expansion where named. A geometric reaction does not additionally require phase REACTION; C does not additionally require phase ROTATION. These labels cannot introduce a second undocumented veto. Age is descriptive; episode timers provide time authority.
At every dispatch: admit/derive all permitted prefix data; update context/phase/landmarks; process expiry and existing-object invalidations; revise/create/arm episodes; evaluate eligible triggers; select/issue; publish view/material changes. Equal-time expiry precedes issue. Newly updated 1h context is checked at birth, arm and trigger for A (must UP for LONG), B (must not DOWN), C (must BALANCED). If the context becomes forbidden during an existing unissued attempt, withdraw that attempt before a coincident trigger. After issue, context change is counterevidence; only the explicit §9 terminal predicates retire the guidance.

MarketView publishes **observed_context** separately from **expected_direction**, principal scenario, alternative, known levels, cutoffs/age and limits. Total priority table:
| First matching condition | Expected direction | Principal scenario / alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Required 15m/1h context unavailable | UNAVAILABLE | Named blockers; no invented alternative levels. |
| Valid armed/triggered scenarios in opposite directions, or an ongoing call opposed by a valid armed scenario | UNCERTAIN | Both frozen scenario IDs and conditions; current call remains distinct. |
| One ongoing recommendation, no opposite armed scenario | Its LONG→UP / SHORT→DOWN | Conditional continuation toward original target while original premise survives; label entry status and current counterevidence. |
| Only one direction among valid armed/triggered candidates | That direction, conditional | Choose A>B>C then creation/ID. If K is crossed on an eligible future minute and premise survives → T; otherwise no call. A trigger whose geometry failed is no longer a valid armed scenario. |
| No such scenario; 1h BALANCED and phase COMPRESSION or ROTATION | BALANCED | If live box: rotation inside [L,U], exit alternatives at frozen edges. Without box: no qualified actionable structure, no invented numerical boundary. |
| Every other assessable combination (including 1h BALANCED + 15m EXPANSION) | UNCERTAIN | State observed direction/phase but no supported prospective plan; list missing candidate antecedent. |

Scenario horizons use family expected durations and remaining lifetime, not a permanent two-hour forecast. Scenario invalidation references its frozen V/setup boundary, expiry and context-forbidden withdrawal; view is recomputed, never a retrospective interpretation. Qualitative support does not imply probability. The table is exhaustive across assessable combinations and does not force UP from a context-only UP reading.
Cycle coverage is meaningful but limited: volatility/structure phase influences box birth and adverse-expansion rules. It does not fully settle the Owner's interest in predictive periodic/long-cycle analysis; that unmet capability remains explicit with the bounded missing-rule study in §2.

## 6. Episode/box ownership and three candidate families
The rules below are LONG; SHORT is exact price-axis reflection: map price P→-P, high→-low and low→-high before applying the LONG predicates, then convert back. This defines mirrored inequalities/min/max without ad hoc choices.
At startup, qualification-history unavailable is INIT (neither false nor true); the first assessable qualifying event is the named initial birth. Each qualifying event produces one persistent episode/attempt ID from version, instrument, family/direction, owner ID and source bar ID. Birth, arm, revision, gate-rejection, withdrawal, consumption and expiry are journalled. Candidates terminal after first trigger evaluation, even if rejected by geometry/priority/conflict/slot; no later same-attempt target rescue. An already armed attempt persists until its first eligible trigger or the named withdrawal/expiry. At every intermediate dispatch a forbidden context withdraws it, not merely at an eventual trigger. Maximum one pending A per direction and one box owner; B/C have independent per-direction attempt budgets for that owner.
An arm is published at its actual dispatch time. Eligible trigger minutes must **start at/after arm publication**, be admitted later than its cursor, and be complete/fresh. This is stricter than merely after the anchor's market end. A straddling-arm bar cannot trigger; contact with V in it withdraws as ARM_CONTACT_TIME_AMBIGUOUS rather than pretending post-arm sequence. All setup extrema/scales are from the named complete input cutoff. Equal highs/lows use the most recent bar for reaction anchors, but pivot ties remain invalid.

### A. Continuation after reaction
Define qualification Q_A per direction: 1h context allowed, ER6≥0.55 and signed six-transition displacement≥1.5 S15, using seven endpoint bars. The first available true observation can create an episode. Thereafter new birth requires a Q_A=false observation after the preceding episode is terminal, followed by a newly observed false→true transition while no pending A exists. True qualification during a pending episode never queues a replacement. At most one pending episode per direction. Required gaps reset the latch but demand fresh contiguous warmup. Changing a rolling-window hash or expiry alone does not renew an episode. This explicit false→true renewal can omit some uninterrupted-trend opportunities; it is a coverage convention to diagnose, not global historical high-water.
Freeze impulse A=min low of seven bars, B=max high, S15/z and source IDs at birth. Watch the next 1–8 complete 15m bars for at least one lower close and reaction R<B-0.25 S15 but R>A+z. Before any qualifying reaction, a high>B+z spends the episode; no continuous re-anchoring of its impulse.
Arm if reaction exists, 1h remains allowed, and no opposite EXPANSION. Anchor=most recent reaction bar attaining R; K=anchor.high; V=R-z. Before trigger a lower clean R>A+z may revise anchor/K/V without extending the original two-hour episode deadline. New forbidden context/opposite expansion/close≤A+z withdraws before trigger.
Trigger: eligible later complete 1m close≥K+tick with low>V. Contact V in that minute rejects as TRIGGER_CONTACT_AMBIGUOUS (pre-issue order cannot be invented). Freeze nearest opposing target at this trigger, including B's owned zone. No destination → NO_TARGET. At issue freeze geometry and original hard horizon. Expected 30–180m; thesis hard deadline 4h from issue.

### Shared qualified compression box (not a B attempt)
Birth requires all box predicates: current phase COMPRESSION; prior 16-bar ER16≤0.35; width 2–6 S15; **both** at least two distinct upper-edge touch bars and two lower-edge touch bars; creation-bar close inside [L,U]. Prior box bounds use the 16 complete bars excluding creation; ER16 uses those bars plus their preceding close. Freeze L/U/M,S15,z, source IDs and four-hour expiry. On initial startup grant one unconsumed birth token to the first assessable compression episode and use its first qualifying box geometry. Thus a just-started contraction with insufficient touches can mature into the first box without another phase reset. Once an owner exists, no replacement while live. A subsequent box requires a complete COMPRESSION=false observation after the preceding owner retires, then a newly observed false→true compression transition granting one birth token for that compression episode. Create its box at the first later/current bar satisfying all box predicates while COMPRESSION remains true; otherwise discard the token when compression ends. A transition during a live owner never queues its replacement; expiry alone cannot recreate the same range. Record reset/transition/birth bars; no sliding edges or global highest-high requirement.
Each box has separate B_LONG/B_SHORT and C_LONG/C_SHORT attempted flags. A B attempt starts on first eligible outside close; a C attempt starts on first reclaim-geometry event. It consumes its family/direction flag even if context/gates later reject it. Returning inside can cancel B while preserving the box for C. Box retires on expiry/required gap, or an opposite-side far-edge **close** during an existing B attempt; a wick penetration/reclaim alone does not destroy it. This last retirement cancels unissued attempts, not a previously issued call's immutable criteria. No ambiguous low-based general destruction rule.
Processing at a close: timer/gap retirement first; derive existing B cancellation and opposite-edge retirement flags together from the pre-dispatch attempt state, then apply them atomically; a returned-inside bar preserves owner; identify new B/C geometry against those same frozen edges; then apply context, arm and triggers. A bar penetrating both far edges cannot originate C (BOTH_EDGE_TEST_AMBIGUOUS). Retirement requires no later callback to resurrect an owner.

### B. Compression exit with retest
First complete 15m close>U+z creates B_LONG attempt (only once per owner/direction); context must not DOWN at birth/arm/trigger. It cannot issue on break bar. Next four complete 15m bars must give a retest low in [U-z,U+z], close>U, low>L. A close<U-z first cancels this upward attempt; close<L-z also retires its box. Neither a mere low through L nor a returned-inside C reclaim is automatic box retirement. Attempt deadline=min(box expiry, break publication+1h).
Arm first qualifying retest: K=max(U+tick,retest.close), V=retest.low-z. Trigger on later eligible 1m close≥K+tick and low>V. Opposite local EXPANSION or forbidden 1h withdraws before trigger. Target=nearest eligible opposing landmark ahead; if absent, use owned projection U+(U-L), explicitly PROJECTED_BOX_WIDTH; a nearer landmark caps it. Expected 30–240m; hard thesis 6h. No retest/chasing substitute.

### C. Failed exit of a compression-qualified box
While that owner is live, reclaim geometry=complete 15m low<L-z, close>L+z and close<M, with no opposite-edge penetration. Create C_LONG attempt only once per owner/direction. 1h must BALANCED at birth/arm/trigger and no local directional EXPANSION (either direction) at birth; adverse EXPANSION withdraws later. It need not be phase ROTATION. This is not a general strategy for all lateral markets.
Arm K=reclaim.close,V=reclaim.low-z. Trigger eligible later 1m close≥K+tick and low>V. Close<L-z before trigger withdraws. Target=min(midpoint M, nearest eligible opposing near edge strictly above trigger), or M when no such landmark; price already inside an opposing zone blocks it. No assumed path to the opposite edge. Expected 15–90m; hard thesis 2h; attempt expires min(box expiry,reclaim publication+30m). A new reclaim after rejected/withdrawn attempt cannot renew it without a new owner under the reset rule.

## 8. Structural area versus current admissible prices
Direction d=+1 LONG/-1 SHORT. At trigger choose/round original V/T and freeze S15. Structural area LONG=[max(V+tick,trigger_close-0.25S15),min(T-tick,trigger_close+0.25S15)]; SHORT mirrors. An empty area rejects. This is an immutable geometric container, **not** a promise every price within it is acceptable.
For price p: G=10000*d*(T-p)/p; Q=10000*d*(p-V)/p. With pinned round-trip adequacy envelope K: require G>K, Q>0, (G-K)/(Q+K)≥1.2. The **current admissible set** is tick prices in the structural area satisfying this predicate AND sufficient remaining time, thesis, data and event conditions. Display structural area and current acceptable bounds distinctly. For constant K, LONG upper bound=(T+1.2V)/(2.2*(1+K/10000)); SHORT lower bound=(T+1.2V)/(2.2*(1-K/10000)). Intersect, round inward to ticks and verify original predicate at endpoints; K≥10000 or invalid input unavailable. Variable-cost profile uses direct predicate/monotone tick search, not an incorrect constant-cost shortcut.
At every reassessment and hypothetical entry opening price, use the same chosen method's predicate. Never grant entry merely because p is in the structural container. No later high/low/close can rescue an opening entry.
Primary HISTORICAL_BASE adequacy: fees 5bps/leg plus combined spread/slippage allowance 2bps/leg, K=14bps. Stress reprices existing primary hypothetical paths with allowance 5bps/leg, K=20bps **for outcome sensitivity only**; it cannot silently change calls/primary admissible sets. A different emission cost gate is a separately versioned method/profile. Declared assumptions, not verified personal fee rates.
LIVE_QUOTED: p is ask/bid, already incorporating entry side-spread. K includes pinned 5bps fees per leg, 2bps slippage per leg, and prospective exit half-spread budget equal to 10000*(ask-bid)/(2*midpoint). Do not add entry half/full spread a second time. Costs remain indicative; quote size/impact and human leverage are outside scope. Alternative fees/slippage are explicit hashed profiles, never account inference. Live quote K can change the current acceptable bounds, not original V/T/structural area.
Stops round away from trigger entry, targets toward it; after rounding recompute area/predicates. Freeze geometry at issue. No target extension/stop widening to pass a gate. Future funding uncertainty disclosed independently, not a known future signal. Original evaluation criteria stay frozen.
One trigger evaluation consumes its attempt. Candidates with both directions actionable → reject CONFLICTED; same direction choose A>B>C then creation/ID. Slot already occupied after existing-call terminal processing → reject SLOT_OCCUPIED, recording suppressed episodes and duration. At most one ongoing **recommendation**, not one human or synthetic position. Retired guidance frees the slot even while a hypothetical exit remains pending. No quota or forced-call manufacture.

## 9. Persistent recommendation, entry and post-publication boundaries
Candidate WATCH→ARMED→TRIGGERED→ISSUED/REJECTED/WITHDRAWN/EXPIRED. Call entry AVAILABLE/CLOSED/UNVERIFIED is separate from thesis ONGOING/TARGET_REACHED/INVALIDATED/TIME_EXPIRED/UNASSESSABLE/**RETIRED**. RETIRED has reason THESIS_FAILED or STALLED and is terminal guidance withdrawal, not an assertion a human sold. No terminal state reopens; history remains.
Freeze at issue: d,V,T,structural area,S15,episode/model/config/evidence, issue clock/cursor, original expected duration and fixed hard deadline. Revisions update current entry/guidance/reasons, not original geometry/outcome criteria. At entry-now show remaining clock time and duration window [family minimum,min(original expected upper,remaining)] only when remaining≥family minimum. Otherwise close new entry as TOO_LATE; never extend deadline. Minimum new-entry residual is 30m A/B,15m C.
A price temporarily outside current admissible set, an event restriction or unavailable live quote can close entry without retiring a still-assessable thesis. It may reopen under the same ID after fresh adequate evidence only while enough time remains and no terminal outcome occurred. Required monitoring gap/stale trade evidence retires as UNASSESSABLE; later data never establish unseen safety. Restart gap overlapping thesis life also UNASSESSABLE; old alerts never replay as new actionable calls.

### Contacts and progress
Certified post-issue OHLC contacts require a complete 1m interval whose **start≥actual issue publication time**. Never use the detection bar or a later-received interval starting before issue as a certified success/failure. A straddling-issue interval whose high/low can touch original T/V is CONTACT_TIME_AMBIGUOUS and makes bar-only thesis UNASSESSABLE (unless actual timestamped trade evidence resolves it; quotes only update current side-price adequacy, not all missed trade contacts). A straddling interval without such contact can update freshness, but does not contribute post-issue progress extrema. Arm boundary uses §6's similarly strict trigger rule.
For wholly post-issue complete bars, T/V simultaneous contact → UNASSESSABLE with ambiguous contact flags/bounds, never target-first; a single certified contact terminates guidance TARGET_REACHED/INVALIDATED. ### Guidance versus hypothetical path
Issue recommendation and revisions belong to the causal method. Hypothetical path/evaluation is a separate simulation over price evidence; it never keeps recommendation slot occupied, changes future calls or claims a real fill. One primary first-entry path per call; no repeated entries on every reopening. After modeled entry, subsequent entry-status closures do not retroactively void that entry. Only recommendation terminal/explicit exit guidance initiates that hypothetical exit process.
Primary entry delay=60s after actual issue publication. Candidate entry boundaries are UTC minute starts at/after ceil-minute(issue+delay). At each boundary s require an assessable nonterminal call, entry-enabled conditions and residual time known from the **pre-open admitted dispatch prefix**, meaning the last causally available method state after any due timers at s (freshness/expiry first), never a state inferred from the opening bar's later close; then verify the observed opening price itself against the primary structural/current-cost admissible predicate. Availability of the opening print may be established by later bar data for this declared hypothetical simulation, but its later high/low/close cannot grant the entry. This is an opening-price model, not measured human timing/queue or perfect contemporaneous receipt.
Only complete minute bars whose start≥issue publication are usable for an opening model. Delay=0 at an exact minute permits that new minute's open only under declared order: expiry/context/issue dispatch first, hypothetical open second. No issue from that minute's future close can enter at its own open. If no qualifying opening while call entry is available, NO_ENTRY. ### Exit/contacts and pending-exit collisions

|---|---|

| Protective T/V plus guidance received during that minute | Guidance starts pending exit; protective contact in that fully observed minute remains active. If both protection levels touched, ambiguity remains. |

### One-unit normalization (not human sizing)
