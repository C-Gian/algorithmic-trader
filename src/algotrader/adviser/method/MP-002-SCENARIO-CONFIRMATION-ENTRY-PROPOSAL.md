## 1. Decision, evidence and limits
Adopt one testable hypothesis: A continuation confirmation and its usable entry need not occur at the same price. A can wait for a subsequent observed return to a bounded reaction corridor after the existing confirmation. Structural interpretation persists independently of economic entry eligibility for A/B/C. B/C entry rules are unchanged.

Evidence: [original report](evidence/WP-009-OWNER-BACKTEST-A.json), [dossier](evidence/WP-009-OWNER-BACKTEST-A-DOSSIER/SUMMARY.md), [Astra dossier review](WP-009-OWNER-BACKTEST-A-DOSSIER-ASTRA-REVIEW.md), and [original method](MP-001-INTEGRATED-METHOD-PROPOSAL.md).
The dossier's 32 geometric rejections are incompatible at minimum confirmation: 14 have no acceptable price for the supplied T/V pair, 18 have economic prices before confirmation; six inside-zone blocks are separate. This is about produced September objects, not every possible trade, future target or period. Only two trigger-time structural areas were economically nonempty: 18 algebraic regions are not 18 usable historical entries.

Director accepts the dossier as sufficient diagnostic evidence for a design proposal, with Astra's qualifications:
- 32 reconstructed targets, six coherent inside-zone reasons, only three uniquely attributable original blocking zones; “38/38 target/zone identities” is not established.
- No future contamination identified in this modeled-complete-prefix, zero-delay run; the extraction is not a general live/late-admission validator.
- Original journal/cache provenance is executor evidence; Astra independently checked supplied arithmetic/zone sets, not an independent re-export.
- Fixed-target algebra does not prove every price-dependent target impossible. A narrower structural intersection cannot rescue any of the observed negative confirmation tests.
- Cost envelope remains a convention; no evidence here validates it as an actual personal execution rate.
No additional extraction, replay, parameter tournament or literature census is needed for this proposal. Acceptance is not economic validation or an assertion that all implementation defects are excluded.

The professional move/reaction/continuation concept is already grounded by MP-001 sources. This precise post-confirmation return rule is a new deterministic ADAPTATION/CONVENTION, not a source-proven BTC edge. Adverse selection and short human entry windows can reject it.

## 3. Independent structural scenario lifecycle
At each structurally determined family birth create a stable scenario ID linked to structural owner and child entry attempt. Birth/reset/zone eligibility never depend on economic entry/call termination. A WATCH scenario says “if a clean reaction and recovery confirm, continuation toward B”; after arm it names K and V and the remaining deadline. B says “if retest and later recovery confirm, continuation toward the box projection, with obstacles”; C says “if reclaim and later recovery confirm, rotation toward M, with obstacles.” No calibrated probability or unconditional UP from observed context.

Scenario fields: family/direction, owner; WATCH/ARMED/CONFIRMED/TERMINAL; antecedent and activation timestamp/cursor; structural destination, eligible opposing obstacles; structural invalidation; creation, actual knowledge/publishing cutoff; original expiry; counterevidence; terminal reason; linked entry attempt/call. Do not store an economic acceptable interval or cost-dependent label as structural evidence.

A narrative destination is frozen B; B is frozen box projection; C is frozen M. Conservative economic target may be nearer and is displayed separately. Reaching an intermediate economic cap closes entry/call under its rules but does not claim the narrative destination was reached. Scenario continues only while its own premise/time/data remain valid.

Scenario termination before structural confirmation:
- original setup deadline, required gap/staleness/restart gap, or frozen owner retirement where that retirement invalidates this unissued structural premise;
- existing family context-forbidden/adverse-expansion withdrawal and named price-premise boundaries;
- after arm, contact with frozen current V in an eligible complete 1m interval invalidates the scenario; straddling-arm contact remains unassessable;
- reaching its frozen narrative destination in a certified complete interval after scenario publication; a straddling interval with potentially relevant contact is explicitly unassessable, never a certified success.
Before arm use existing setup withdrawal/spend rules; do not invent V.
After structural confirmation no anchor/R/K/V revisions. Confirmed scenario termination is independent of call existence: frozen V contact, narrative destination contact, family price-premise failure, required coverage loss, or fixed family hard deadline from confirmation. A/ B/ C deadlines are confirmation+4h/6h/2h. Box/entry-attempt expiry alone no longer destroys a confirmed narrative; retain bounded frozen refs. Context change becomes counterevidence for the confirmed scenario, while existing pre-issue entry context gates can still withdraw its entry attempt. Confirmed-scenario stalled test uses maximum favorable complete-minute close displacement from the confirmation close at half its fixed horizon, threshold 0.5 confirmation-time S15; it is separately recorded from call progress.
Simultaneous destination/invalidation contacts are unassessable. Record ambiguity, not target-first.
Call issue/retirement/evaluation cannot mutate structural scenario fields. A conservative call target can terminate guidance while the independent narrative survives. Scenario termination blocks new entry and retires linked ongoing guidance explicitly as SCENARIO_TERMINAL when not already terminal, with the underlying terminal reason passed to the separate evaluator under §6/§7 (coverage loss censors, other guidance terminals request the declared delayed exit); call termination never feeds back into the scenario.
### Structural discovery owner versus child entry attempt — B1 closure

Confirmation, immediate issue, WAIT, economic/selection rejection and call retirement do NOT release it. A confirmed scenario's independent stalled terminal does not accelerate discovery release; original setup expiry still releases if no earlier named release occurred.
After structural release, require an assessable Q_A=false observed at or after release, followed by a newly observed false-to-true transition while no discovery owner exists. A false observed strictly before release is not banked. At the release dispatch, expiry/terminal processing precedes Q_A observation; false there can reset, true there cannot create a replacement. A gap resets to INIT with fresh contiguous warmup; initial startup behavior remains MP-001. This is a categorical renewal change, not unchanged v0.2 attempt-terminal renewal.
The structural owner remains occupied after entry issue/rejection until that release. Its confirmed scenario can outlive discovery release until its own fixed terminal; it grants no new entry budget. A's own B zone is eligible only during structural-owner life and until its named 15m far-edge break; release retires zone eligibility regardless of entry mode/cost. Frozen narrative references survive for audit, not as a renewed opposing zone.
Box discovery/birth/retirement remains structural MP-001. B/C structural discovery flags are consumed by the first qualifying structural birth for that family/direction, never reset by costs/calls. The opposite-edge close rule uses existence of the structurally born B episode until its structural returned-inside/forbidden-context/expiry/price terminal, ignoring child economic rejection. This explicitly prevents an economic rejection from altering later box retirement. Each child entry is still one-shot; no additional B/C entry policy.
State bounds cannot evict by cost, call count or success. A birth cadence is at most one per completed 15m boundary per direction; <=4h confirmed lifetime plus unconfirmed owner yields a conservative capacity 18 scenarios/direction (including boundary headroom), plus retained audit refs for the one guidance slot. B/C: at most one box birth per completed 15m boundary, four structural family/direction episodes/box, <=1h pre-confirmation plus <=6h confirmed life; conservative capacity 128 B/C scenarios covers 7h*4/15m + live-owner/boundary headroom. Terminal history lives in the journal, not active state. Breaching these capacities is an explicit invariant error, never economic eviction. Later implementation must test the bound, not silently delete a supported scenario.
Structural IDs/lineage use structural rule identity + instrument + owner/source/cutoff, independent of execution-cost profile. Full record envelopes may carry different complete behavior IDs; compare normalized structural lineage/content (excluding economic envelope, child IDs and journal sequence), not incompatible global configuration hashes. Structural forecasts and sampling cutoffs remain invariant. Economic child creation must not change source-derived structural tie IDs.

A rejected B/C entry remains consumed; its structural scenario may persist to its independent structural deadline without ever issuing another call. This is an explanation, not an alternative entry engine. Retain existing A spent-before-reaction order and structural false-to-true qualification, with the explicit release/reset-origin delta above; the 11 OHLC dual-condition bars do not justify an intrabar reorder.

## 5. A confirmation and immutable entry container
A birth, reaction/arm/revision and first confirming minute remain MP-001. Later fresh complete 1m close meets K+tick for LONG, reflected for SHORT; low>V for LONG/high<V for SHORT. Minute start >= actual arm publication and admitted after its cursor. A confirmation-minute V contact prevents clean confirmation/entry. A scenario whose V was published before the entire interval is INVALIDATED by that certified contact; a straddling-V interval makes it UNASSESSABLE. The order of recovery versus contact is not invented.

At that first confirmation freeze actual reaction R, anchor threshold K_trigger, tick-rounded V, then-current S15 and source refs. Round V away from intended entry as before. Resolve conservative T from the actual confirmation-close zone set before cost filtering; all newly eligible structural updates/breaks are applied first. Round T toward price. No target/inside-zone → terminal entry rejection (NO_TARGET / AT_OPPOSING_AREA); structural scenario stays governed by §3. All containing zones and the selected attribution are recorded.

LONG fixed reaction corridor:
L=max(ceil_tick(R), V+tick)
U=min(floor_tick(K_trigger), T-tick).
SHORT reflects the price axis, then rounds inward in market prices. Prices equal to V or T are excluded. This corridor represents the already-observed reaction-to-anchor structure, not a band invented around the current close.
Immediate confirmation entry also remains possible: freeze the existing MP-001 confirmation-close structural band I0 with confirmation-time S15 and intersect it with valid V/T prices. Preserve its exact v0.2 width; do not widen I0 to obtain an entry.

Immediate I0 is the v0.2 band around confirmation close, evaluated at actual historical close/live side price. A single-tick valid corridor is allowed; no new minimum width. R>K LONG is empty, not swapped. The return corridor is not unioned with I0: once selected, issued geometry uses only that mode. Nongeometric veto precedence at confirmation versus temporary waiting blockers is a declared asymmetry of this version.
WAIT starts only with a valid current economic corridor and live quotes if required. Only an already admitted WAIT may recover from later transient live-cost/quote restrictions. No-target, contacts or broken structure do not route into waiting. This version cannot rescue globally empty geometry.

The original setup deadline bounds waiting. No extension at confirmation/return. A hard expiry is confirmation+4h for both its confirmed scenario and eventual call, not return issue+4h. This is an explicit delta: waiting never buys a fresh full horizon. At issue require at least 30m residual and display [30m,min(180m,residual)]; subsequent residual closure follows MP-001. Entry delay starts at actual issue, not confirmation. Unissued confirmations at/after evaluation-window end cannot produce calls.

## 6. Return waiting, contacts, costs and new obstacles
WAIT_PRICE is not an adviser call, does not occupy recommendation slot, does not alert “enter now”, and creates no hypothetical path. Display “Scenario confirmed; waiting for a usable price”, remaining original setup time, corridor, obstacle, V/T and limitations.

Use the nominal historical K_cost or the pinned profile's current live quoted cost formula; no historical quote invention. At confirmation, absent/unusable live quotes cannot establish a usable execution region: do not start RETURN on a fabricated K. In live mode that confirmation ends entry as EXECUTION_UNVERIFIED, while structural scenario remains. This preserves existing one-shot execution policy.

### Cap activation and recoverable live economics — B3/B4 closure
Every later dispatch orders timer/coverage/scenario terminals before entry; process newly admitted complete intervals against levels active for their domains, then publish new structural cap updates. Fixed V and T_confirm never move away. Preserve confirmation zone IDs and a seen-later eligible zone-ID set with actual publication/cursor. A zone is NEW for cap revision only at its first eligibility after confirmation; previously broken/retired objects never reenter, and a new ID is new evidence. A still-active zone currently behind price is not permanently deleted: current containing-zone checks always use the eligible structural set, with MP-001 15m-close break and retirement semantics. Passing a level at one quote is not a structural break.
For a new LONG opposing zone whose near edge n is >V and <T_current, lower cap to inward-rounded n; SHORT reflects structural inequalities. Each new cap stores effective actual publication/cursor and cannot subsequently widen, even when its source retires/breaks. If new cap is <=current structural close or the execution-side price LONG (>= for SHORT), end child entry as CAP_NOT_AHEAD_NOW, never target-success. Compare each available current price; absent required execution sample prevents issue but cannot fabricate a past contact. If n<=V but far edge>V LONG, block all corridor ticks inside that zone and retain the cap; never clip to/below V to fabricate risk. Its dynamic eligible-zone exclusion follows ordinary break/retirement and may be temporary. If no corridor tick remains outside this still-active zone, wait is BLOCKED_BY_ZONE until break/retirement/normal terminal, not permanently erased by a temporary zone exclusion. If the monotone cap's V/T clipping itself empties fixed structural corridor, terminate EMPTY_RETURN_CORRIDOR.
For each newly complete bar, certify contacts only against V and the cap already active at its interval START. An interval wholly after confirmation with high/low crossing that already-active cap ends entry as PRE_ENTRY_TARGET_CONTACT; crossing both V/cap is ambiguous/unassessable. If an interval straddles confirmation or any cap's activation and touches the newly introduced cap, end entry conservatively as CAP_ACTIVATION_CONTACT_AMBIGUOUS (or PRE_ENTRY_CONTACT_TIME_AMBIGUOUS for initial confirmation). Do NOT label it target reached. The interval that introduces a new cap at its own end is such an activation interval; its earlier high cannot be a retroactive hit. If no relevant new-cap contact occurs and current price remains before the cap, its close may be reassessed at that dispatch against the now-known cap, with no high/low chronology claim.
Confirmation-minute contact with freshly selected T excludes BOTH A modes under §5. A target cap is an entry boundary/obstacle, not scenario narrative destination. Scenario destination contacts use their own independently published level and interval domain.

Economic region and structural corridor are distinct. Structural clipping to empty is permanent for this attempt. During an already valid WAIT, a current LIVE_QUOTED K making the economic intersection empty is TEMPORARY_COST_BLOCKED; quote/connection loss is UNVERIFIED/BLOCKED. Cost recovery may restore eligibility within unchanged corridor and surviving clocks, never issue while blocked. New cap leaving geometry but no current live economic ticks follows that same temporary cost rule. HISTORICAL_BASE K is fixed: empty economic intersection under a monotone cap is terminal NO_ECONOMIC_RETURN_REGION, because subsequent facts cannot widen the cap/corridor or lower K. Initial empty economic intersection still cannot start WAIT in either profile.

A later return event requires a fresh complete minute starting at/after confirmation publication, admitted strictly later than confirmation cursor, with no relevant V/cap/activation contact, and a close in the fixed corridor. Historical sample is that close; live close AND fresh ask/bid must satisfy the current corridor, eligible-zone exclusion and economy. An intrabar corridor touch is never first issue. Calendar/dislocation/quote/service restriction may keep an already accepted WAIT; the first fully actionable event alone enters selection. Original setup deadline, forbidden preissue context/adverse-expansion, monitoring loss and structural terminals close waiting before selection. Slot/conflict/priority then consume the first actionable return candidate with no later retry.

## 7. Post-issue call and hypothetical evaluator
Freeze at actual issue: selected I0 or return container, fixed V, current conservative T, issue-time/current profile and causal refs, confirmation ID/time, confirmation-anchored A hard expiry and S15. Let tc=actual confirmation publication, ti=actual issue publication, H=tc+4h for A. Entry requires H-now>=30m and WAIT ends at original setup expiry. A scenario progress timer is tc+2h, measured from confirmation close and S15_confirmation. Call progress timer is ti+(H-ti)/2, measured from actual issue-reference close and S15_issue. Both compare max favorable certified complete-minute CLOSE displacement against 0.5 their frozen scale; missing coverage is UNASSESSABLE. The first explicit scenario/call terminal wins. Minimum residual is an eligibility filter, not a guaranteed holding duration. B/C immediate confirmation=issue; their original +6h/+2h hard horizons and midpoint checks coincide naturally. Original confirmation T and any cap history remain inspectable.
After issue retain MP-001 protective contacts, call progress, premises, residual-time entry closure, live connectivity and call persistence, subject to the explicit A confirmation-anchored hard deadline and independent scenario-terminal rule. A later obstacle is visible counterevidence/current-entry restriction, not a retrospective replacement of the frozen outcome target. Do not extend T/stop/hard horizon.
Same-call entry may close/reopen according to existing rules; this never reissues a new call or creates a second evaluator entry. A terminal call cannot reopen. “One attempt” limits first issuance, not legitimate availability updates under its stable ID.

The evaluator stays separate: calls identical when evaluator is disabled; no retrospective reaction/confirmation entry. Primary first-entry path starts >= ceil-minute(actual_issue+60s); sensitivities 0/120 use those same calls. Explicit evaluator delta: A HORIZON_ONLY exit request uses the frozen H=confirmation+4h, then existing exit delay/alignment, NOT issue+4h. B/C use their existing frozen hard deadlines. It remains conditional on the same primary entry, not a direction-alpha comparison. Pre-open state/timers plus opening-price admissibility, protection, guidance delay, gap/ambiguity/funding/censoring and one-unit accounting remain MP-001. WAIT_PRICE creates neither NO_ENTRY trade nor an artificial loss; count it in the candidate funnel, not call/path denominators.
Warmup/tail remain unscored and no historical catch-up calls become live alerts. Existing evaluation-start reset must remove pre-window pending ENTRY attempts; structural warmup evidence remains context and is labelled. No issue whose confirmation occurred in warmup is scored as a new-window return rescue.
